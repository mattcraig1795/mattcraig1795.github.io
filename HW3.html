Matt's Roadtrip Bucket List

I remember one random day in 2015 I had decided to go on a cross country roadtrip with a bunch of friends and just let the vibes and wind tell us where to go. And so me and 4 of my best friends hopped in my mom's 2011 Chrysler Town N Country minivan and started our adventure. That roadtrip opened my eyes to how I experience travel and now Roadtrips are the lifeblood of my existence. Roadtripping all through the US and Canada has rewarded me with some of the best memories of my life.

What Makes a Good Road Trip?

  • Finding weird roadside attractions, like the world's largest Pistachio!
  • When nothing goes according to plan and you gain experiences you never expected.
  • Hiking and exploring national parks and enjoying the raw natural beauty of our planet.
  • Taking the scenic route and not being too constrained on time.
  • Meeting new people and trying local experiences from places you never heard of.

My Top 5 Dream Road Trips

  1. Roadtrip through Alaska
  2. Vancouver to Montreal Coast-to-Coast Roadtrip
  3. Roadtrip through the South from Lousiana to Virginia
  4. Northeastern US Roadtrip
  5. New Zealand Roadtrip.
